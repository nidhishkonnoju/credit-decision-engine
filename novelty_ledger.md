# Novelty Ledger

Lin and Wang (2025), "SHAP Stability in Credit Risk Management," *Risks*, 13(238), study how SHAP feature rankings vary across XGBoost random seeds and use rank stability to distinguish reliable from unstable explanations. This repository adopts that seed-only stability design and applies it to the audited bureau-plus-bank underwriting-tier dataset. The project-specific addition is the downstream operational layer: it filters applicant explanations with an explicit stability rule, maps retained factors to the five Cs of credit, renders all five categories including empty ones, and measures the effect of filtering through a fixed-sample ablation and coverage evaluation. The repository does not claim that the stability calculation itself is new relative to Lin and Wang.
